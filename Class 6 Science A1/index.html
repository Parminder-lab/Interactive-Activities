What is Photosynthesis?

The magical food-making process in green plants!

Photosynthesis comes from two Greek words: 'Photo' meaning light, and 'Synthesis' meaning putting together.

Green plants are nature's chefs! Unlike animals and humans who depend on other food sources, plants prepare their own food in their leaves using simple ingredients from nature.

Did you know?

Leaves act like tiny food factories. They contain a green pigment called Chlorophyll which traps sunlight!

The 4 Essential Ingredients

1. Sunlight

Captured by chlorophyll in the leaves to provide energy for the chemical reaction.

2. Water

Absorbed by the roots from the soil and transported upward through stem vessels.

3. Carbon Dioxide

Taken from the air through tiny microscopic pores on the leaves called stomata.

4. Chlorophyll

The green pigment inside plant cells that traps sunlight energy.
