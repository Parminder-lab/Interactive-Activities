Topic 1: Modes of Nutrition

Autotrophic vs. Heterotrophic Nutrition

All living organisms require energy to carry out life processes. Based on how they obtain food, nutrition is divided into two primary modes: Autotrophic (self-nourishing) and Heterotrophic (other-nourished).

🌱

Autotrophic Nutrition

Auto = Self | Trophe = Nourishment

  • Organisms synthesize their own organic food from simple inorganic materials (CO2, H2O) using sunlight.
  • Contains photosynthetic pigments like Chlorophyll.
  • Examples: Green plants, Euglena, Cyanobacteria (Blue-green algae).
🦁

Heterotrophic Nutrition

Hetero = Other | Trophe = Nourishment

  • Organisms cannot prepare their own food; they depend directly or indirectly on autotrophs.
  • Lacks photosynthetic pigments; complex food is broken down by enzymes.
  • Examples: Animals, Humans, Fungi (Bread mould, Yeast), Amoeba.

Topic 2: Process of Photosynthesis

How Green Plants Make Food

Photosynthesis is the biochemical process by which autotrophs take in substances from the outside (CO2 and H2O) and convert them into stored chemical energy (Carbohydrates) in the presence of sunlight and chlorophyll.

Topic 3: Key Plant Structures

Chlorophyll & Stomata Mechanics

Explore the green organelle that traps sunlight and the microscopic pores that allow plants to breathe and regulate water.

🧪

Role of Chlorophyll

Green Photosynthetic Pigment

1. Location: Chlorophyll pigments are housed inside cell organelles called Chloroplasts in plant leaves.
2. Light Absorption: It absorbs sunlight (specifically blue and red wavelengths) and reflects green light, giving leaves their green color.
3. Energy Transduction: It converts trapped solar light energy into chemical energy during the light phase of photosynthesis.
NCERT Key Fact: Desert plants take up CO2 at night and prepare an intermediate which is acted upon by energy absorbed by chlorophyll during the day!
