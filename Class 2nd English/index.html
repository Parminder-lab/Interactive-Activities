The Fox and the Grapes

Class 2 English Challenge

Tap the flip cards to learn, then take the quiz!

📖 Read the Story

One sunny day, a hungry fox was walking through a garden. He saw a bunch of juicy grapes hanging high on a vine.

“Those grapes look delicious!” said the fox. He jumped to reach them, but they were too high. He tried again and again, but he could not get them.

At last, the tired fox walked away and said, “Those grapes are probably sour!”

Moral: It is easy to dislike what we cannot have.
