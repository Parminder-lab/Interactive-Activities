Which Direction Does Friction Act?

Friction is a resistive force that always opposes motion. It acts parallel to the contact surface in the exact opposite direction to applied push or movement!

Concept 2 • Friction Types

Static vs Sliding vs Rolling Friction

Learn how friction changes when an object is stationary, sliding, or rolling!

HIGHEST

1. Static Friction

Acts on stationary objects trying to move. High initial resistance.

Threshold: HIGH (80 N)
MEDIUM

2. Sliding Friction

Acts when surfaces slide past each other continuously.

Resistance: MEDIUM (40 N)
LOWEST

3. Rolling Friction

Acts when wheels or round objects roll over surfaces.

Resistance: VERY LOW (10 N)
